This notice explains how Laurus Group Limited uses personal information in connection with this website, laurusgroup.co.uk. Podium Vehicle Solutions, the Laurus Operating System (LOS) and PaddockDesk have their own websites and privacy notices.

1. Who we are

Laurus Group Limited is the data controller for the personal information described in this notice.

  • Registered in England and Wales, company number 17295288.
  • Registered office: 316 Warwick Road, Banbury, OX16 1AZ.
  • Registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number ZC246352.
  • Privacy contact: hello@laurusgroup.co.uk.

2. Information we collect

This website has no forms, user accounts, advertising or analytics built into it, and its fonts are served from this website rather than a third party. We collect personal information in two ways:

  • When you email us. If you contact us at one of the email addresses on this website, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything you choose to write or attach.
  • When you visit the website. This website is hosted by Cloudflare. To send each page to your device, Cloudflare receives the details of your request, including your IP address and the information your browser sends with it. To confirm: which Cloudflare features are switched on for this website, for example Web Analytics or bot protection, and what they collect.

3. How we use it, and our lawful basis

  • Replying to your email, and handling any follow-up you ask for. We do this because we have a legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us and in running our business. Where your enquiry is about a service you would like from us, we also rely on taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Delivering this website and keeping it secure. We do this because we have a legitimate interest in providing a website that works and is protected against misuse.
  • Meeting legal obligations, where the law requires us to keep or provide information.

Where we rely on legitimate interests, you can object, as explained in section 9.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. The Laurus Group brands and websites (including Laurus Operating System, PaddockDesk and Podium Vehicle Solutions) are all part of Laurus Group Limited, so passing your enquiry to the right part of the business is not sharing it with another organisation.

  • Website hosting: Cloudflare hosts this website and receives the request details described in section 2.
  • Email: emails sent to our addresses are delivered and stored by our email provider. To confirm: email provider.
  • Professional advisers, such as accountants, lawyers and insurers, where they need the information to advise us.
  • Public authorities, regulators or law enforcement, where the law requires us to share it.

5. International transfers

To confirm: whether Cloudflare or our email provider processes personal information outside the UK, and the safeguards that apply. This section will describe them once confirmed.

6. How long we keep it

  • Ordinary business enquiries that do not lead to a contract, a complaint or a legal obligation: we keep them for 12 months after our last contact with you.
  • Longer where necessary: where information is needed for a contract, for accounting, or to deal with a complaint or legal claim, we keep it for as long as that purpose requires, under our documented retention rules. To confirm: the documented retention rules (periods or criteria) for contracts, accounting records, complaints and legal claims.
  • To confirm: whether any website request information is kept, by whom, and for how long.

7. Cookies

This website's own code does not set cookies or store information in your browser. To confirm: whether any Cloudflare feature switched on for this website sets cookies.

8. Links to other websites

This website links to other Laurus Group websites and to other organisations. Each website has its own privacy notice, which applies when you visit it.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of your personal information;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate information;
  • ask us to delete your information in certain situations;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your information in certain situations;
  • object to how we use your information, including where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • ask for your information in a portable format, where this applies.

To use any of these rights, email hello@laurusgroup.co.uk.

10. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we use your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
