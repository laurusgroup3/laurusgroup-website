Property

Laurus Home

A connected homeowner platform, built on the same foundation as Laurus Trade Quotes — one account for property records, trade quotations, maintenance planning, emergency coordination and membership, all in one place.

Currently in development. Trade quotations are live today at Laurus Trade Quotes — the full homeowner account is being built next.

What Laurus Home Connects

Everything a homeowner needs to manage their property and their trade work, in one connected account.

My Property

Property information, appliances, notes and photographs, kept in one private record.

Quotes & Jobs

Trade quotation enquiries, progress updates and communication, powered by Laurus Trade Quotes.

Maintenance

Planned maintenance tasks, reminders and a full service history for the property.

Documents

Certificates, invoices, reports and manuals, stored privately against the property.

Emergency Assistance

A clear way to request help and track coordination when something needs urgent attention.

Membership

Plan, benefits, renewal and billing, managed from the homeowner's own account.

Membership Plans

Provisional pricing, shown for development preview only — not yet available to purchase.

Laurus Home Basic

Free / £0

A homeowner account, basic property profile, standard quote requests, request tracking and standard emergency enquiries.

Most popular

Laurus Home Plus

£14.99 / month

or £149 annually

Everything in Basic, plus property management, maintenance planning, reminders, service history, property document storage and a maintenance calendar.

Laurus Home Complete

£39.99 / month

or £399 annually

Everything in Plus, plus priority emergency enquiry handling, priority coordination with available tradespeople, emergency request tracking, a priority membership indicator for staff and an annual property maintenance planning review.

Emergency call-outs, attendance, labour and materials are always charged separately, on every plan. Laurus Home Complete is a priority-handling membership, not insurance, not unlimited repair cover, and not a guarantee of engineer availability or arrival time.

Laurus Home is built directly on Laurus Trade Quotes' existing, live quotation platform — a homeowner's trade enquiries and property records will live in one connected account, not two separate systems.
