Product Previews

PaddockDesk and PitstopDash

PaddockDesk

Workshop management software that connects bookings, jobs, technicians, parts and invoicing, so a garage can see every vehicle from check-in to collection in one place.

  • Working in alpha: reception, jobs, technician workflow, vehicle health reports, invoicing and handover.
  • In development: supplier parts ordering. Not yet a live production service.
Explore PaddockDesk (opens the PaddockDesk website in a new tab)

PitstopDash

The customer-facing companion to PaddockDesk. Drivers see their vehicle's health report, maintenance history, advisories and MOT and service reminders on their phone, in plain language.

  • In development: an alpha demo with fictional data, not yet available to customers.
