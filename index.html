PaddockDesk — Workshop Management Software

Bookings, jobs, technicians, vehicle health, parts tracking, invoicing and customer handover in one connected workshop system.

In active alpha development — informed by real workshop operations at Podium Vehicle Solutions.

What PaddockDesk Connects

One system across the whole workshop journey, instead of spreadsheets and separate tools.

Reception & Booking

Vehicles checked in and jobs opened against a single connected record.

Job Card & Technician

Diagnostics, labour, parts and technician progress tracked against one job, on desktop or phone.

Health, Invoice & Handover

A structured vehicle health report, invoice and payment status carried through to collection.

PitstopDash: the customer companion

PaddockDesk is for the garage team. PitstopDash is its customer-facing companion, so drivers can see what the workshop found and what their vehicle needs next.

Vehicle Health Reports

The workshop's health check, item by item, in plain language.

Maintenance History

Past visits and work carried out, kept with the vehicle.

Advisories

Items to keep an eye on before they become urgent.

MOT & Service Reminders

Clear due dates for each vehicle's MOT and next service.

In development: PitstopDash is an alpha demo with fictional data and is not yet available to customers.
